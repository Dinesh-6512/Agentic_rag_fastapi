# Lumina Fit Smart Watch - User Manual

## Device Overview & Specifications
- Model: Lumina-SW20
- Display: 1.43-inch AMOLED high-resolution touch display (466 x 466 pixels, 60Hz)
- Frame Material: Aerospace-grade aluminum alloy with fluoroelastomer quick-release strap
- Water Resistance: 5 ATM / 50 meters water resistance standard (ISO 22810:2010 certified)
- Battery: 300mAh lithium-polymer battery
- Typical Battery Life: Up to 7 days with normal daily use; up to 14 days in Battery Saver Mode
- Sensors: Optical heart rate sensor, pulse oximeter (SpO2), 3-axis accelerometer, gyroscope, geomagnetic sensor
- Connectivity: Bluetooth 5.3 Low Energy (BLE)
- Operating System Compatibility: Android 8.0+ and iOS 13.0+ (requires Lumina Health mobile companion app)

## Charging Instructions
1. Use only the provided magnetic USB charging dock included in the packaging box.
2. Align the two gold-plated magnetic charging pins on the charging dock with the corresponding charging contacts located on the rear casing of the watch.
3. The magnets will snap the dock securely into position.
4. Plug the USB-A end of the cable into a standard 5V/1A or 5V/2A certified wall adapter or a computer USB port.
5. The watch screen will illuminate and display the charging percentage. A full charge from 0% to 100% takes approximately 90 minutes.
6. Note: Do not use fast-charging power adapters exceeding 5V/2.4A as this may damage the internal battery regulator.

## Water Resistance & Environmental Limits
- The Lumina Fit Smart Watch has a water resistance rating of 5 ATM (50 meters). It is safe for recreational swimming in pools or shallow oceans, showering in cool water, and rain.
- Temperature Operating Range: -10°C to 45°C (14°F to 113°F).
- STRICT WARNING - Sauna and Hot Water Restriction: Never wear the watch inside a sauna, steam room, hot tub, thermal spring, or hot shower. High temperatures cause the waterproof silicone gaskets to expand and degrade, allowing steam to penetrate the device.
- High-velocity water sports (such as waterskiing, jet-skiing, or deep scuba diving) exceed the 5 ATM pressure limit and are not supported.
- After swimming in chlorinated or salt water, always rinse the watch thoroughly with fresh tap water and dry it with a soft microfiber cloth.

## Heart Rate & Activity Tracking
- Daily Step and Calorie Tracking: Tracks daily physical movement automatically using internal motion sensors.
- Heart Rate Monitoring: Continuous 24/7 heart rate monitoring with customizable high/low heart rate alerts.
- Sleep Tracking: Automatically records deep sleep, light sleep, REM sleep, and awake periods between 8:00 PM and 10:00 AM.
- Workout Modes: Supports over 30 sports activities including outdoor running, treadmill, pool swimming, cycling, and yoga.

## Troubleshooting & Reset Procedure
- Screen Unresponsive: Press and hold the physical side button for 12 seconds to perform a hardware reboot.
- Bluetooth Sync Fails: Turn off Bluetooth on your smartphone, wait 10 seconds, turn it back on, and re-open the Lumina Health app.
- Factory Reset: On the watch display, swipe down from the home screen, select "Settings" (gear icon) > "System" > "Factory Reset". This clears all on-device historical data and restores the watch to its original out-of-box state.
