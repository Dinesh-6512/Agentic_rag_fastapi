# Lumina Silent Wireless Mouse - User Manual

## Product Specifications
- Model: Lumina-WM10
- Connectivity: Dual-mode Bluetooth 5.2 and 2.4GHz Wireless (USB-A nano receiver included)
- Sensor: Optical tracking sensor with adjustable DPI (800, 1200, 1600, 2400, 4000 DPI)
- Battery: Built-in 500mAh rechargeable lithium-ion battery
- Battery Life: Up to 60 days on a single full charge with average daily usage (8 hours/day)
- Charging Port: USB Type-C (5V / 500mA input)
- Dimensions: 108mm x 62mm x 38mm
- Weight: 85 grams
- Compatibility: Windows 10/11, macOS 10.15+, ChromeOS, Linux, Android 9.0+, and iPadOS

## Bluetooth Pairing Instructions
1. Turn the power switch located on the bottom of the mouse to the "ON" position.
2. Slide the mode switch on the bottom of the mouse to the Bluetooth icon (BT mode).
3. Press and hold the circular "Connect" button for 3 seconds until the top LED indicator flashes blue rapidly. This indicates the mouse has entered pairing mode.
4. Open the Bluetooth settings on your laptop, tablet, or PC.
5. In the list of available devices, select "Lumina Silent Mouse".
6. The LED indicator will turn solid blue for 3 seconds and then turn off, confirming that pairing is complete.

## 2.4GHz USB Receiver Setup
1. Remove the magnetic battery cover on the underside of the mouse to access the USB nano receiver compartment.
2. Take out the USB nano receiver and plug it into an available USB-A port on your computer.
3. Slide the mode switch on the bottom of the mouse to the "2.4G" icon.
4. The LED indicator will glow solid green for 2 seconds. The mouse connects automatically without requiring additional driver installation.

## LED Indicator Meanings
- Rapid Blinking Blue: Mouse is in Bluetooth pairing mode searching for devices.
- Solid Blue (3 seconds): Bluetooth paired and connected successfully.
- Solid Green (2 seconds): 2.4GHz wireless receiver connected successfully.
- Blinking Red: Battery level is below 15%. Recharge immediately with the USB-C cable.
- Solid Red (while plugged in): Mouse battery is actively charging.
- Solid Green (while plugged in): Mouse battery is 100% fully charged.

## Troubleshooting & Factory Reset
- Mouse cursor does not move: Ensure the power switch is ON and battery is charged. Verify that the mode switch matches your connection method (BT or 2.4G).
- Bluetooth connection drops: Remove "Lumina Silent Mouse" from your computer's paired Bluetooth list, restart computer Bluetooth, and repeat the Bluetooth Pairing Instructions.
- Factory Reset Procedure: To clear all stored Bluetooth profiles and reset factory default DPI settings, hold down the Left Mouse Button, Right Mouse Button, and Scroll Wheel Click simultaneously for 5 seconds. The LED will blink white 3 times to confirm the reset.
