# Lumina Wave ANC Headphones - User Manual

## Technical Specifications
- Model: Lumina-HP30
- Speaker Driver: 40mm custom bio-cellulose dynamic drivers
- Frequency Response: 20Hz - 20,000Hz (Hi-Res Audio Certified via 3.5mm cable)
- Impedance: 32 Ohms
- Connectivity: Bluetooth 5.3 and 3.5mm gold-plated auxiliary audio cable
- Bluetooth Range: Up to 15 meters (50 feet) line-of-sight
- Noise Cancellation: Hybrid Active Noise Cancellation (dual internal feedforward and feedback microphones reducing ambient noise by up to 35dB)
- Battery Capacity: 750mAh rechargeable lithium-ion battery
- Battery Playback Time: 
  - Up to 40 hours with Active Noise Cancellation (ANC) turned ON
  - Up to 60 hours with Active Noise Cancellation (ANC) turned OFF
- Quick Charge Support: A 10-minute USB-C charge provides 4 hours of music playback.
- Full Charge Time: Approximately 2 hours from 0% to 100% using a standard 5V/1A charger.
- Weight: 250 grams (ergonomic memory foam protein leather earcups)

## Power & Bluetooth Pairing
1. Press and hold the multi-function Power button on the right earcup for 4 seconds.
2. The LED indicator will alternately flash blue and red, accompanied by an audio chime "Pairing mode".
3. Open your mobile phone, computer, or audio player's Bluetooth menu.
4. Select "Lumina Wave ANC" from the list of discoverable devices.
5. Once connected, the LED indicator will blink blue once every 5 seconds, and a voice prompt will announce "Connected".

## Active Noise Cancellation (ANC) Modes
The dedicated "ANC" button is situated on the lower edge of the left earcup. Press the ANC button sequentially to toggle between three distinct listening modes:
1. ANC Mode (High Noise Cancellation): Blocks background ambient hums, engine noise, and chatter. Ideal for flights, trains, and office environments.
2. Transparency / Ambient Mode: Amplifies surrounding voices and traffic sounds using the external microphones so you can talk without removing the headphones.
3. Normal Mode (ANC Off): Disables noise cancellation circuitry to maximize battery lifespan up to 60 hours.

## Multipoint Dual-Device Connection
1. Pair the headphones with your first device (Device A) using standard Bluetooth pairing.
2. Disable Bluetooth on Device A to allow the headphones to re-enter pairing mode.
3. Turn on Bluetooth on your second device (Device B) and pair with "Lumina Wave ANC".
4. Re-enable Bluetooth on Device A. The headphones will now remain simultaneously connected to both Device A and Device B.
5. Audio will automatically pause on one device if an incoming voice call is received on the other.

## Troubleshooting & Reset Procedure
- No sound from headphones: Ensure the headphones are turned ON, Bluetooth is paired, and the volume on both the headphones and the connected device is raised.
- Wired Mode operation: When using the 3.5mm audio cable, Bluetooth turns off automatically. ANC can still be turned ON in wired mode if battery power remains.
- Hardware Reset: If the headphones freeze or fail to pair, connect the USB-C charging cable to a power source, then press and hold the Power button and Volume Down button simultaneously for 7 seconds. The status LED will flash purple 3 times, indicating memory reset.
